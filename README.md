# Motion-Robust Heart Rate Sensor (MHRS)

## Project Overview

MHRS is a wearable photoplethysmography (PPG) research platform designed to investigate methods for improving heart-rate sensing during movement.

The project will use a low-cost modular prototype to collect raw optical, motion, and contact data. Different sensor configurations will be tested to determine how they affect PPG signal quality and heart-rate reliability during movement.

## Research Areas

- Multi-sensor optical arrays
- Multi-wavelength PPG sensing
- Ambient-light and optical shielding
- Motion-based artifact detection
- Contact and pressure-aware sensing
- Adaptive photodiode/sensor selection
