# Motion-Robust Heart Rate Sensor (MHRS)

## Project Overview

MHRS is a wearable photoplethysmography (PPG) research platform designed
to investigate methods for improving heart-rate sensing during movement.

The project will use a low-cost modular prototype to collect raw optical,
motion, and potentially contact data. We will experimentally compare
different sensing configurations and evaluate their effects on PPG
signal quality and heart-rate reliability.

## Research Areas

- Spatial/multi-sensor PPG
- Multi-wavelength optical sensing
- Ambient-light and optical shielding
- Motion sensing and artifact detection
- Contact/pressure-aware sensing
- Adaptive sensor selection
