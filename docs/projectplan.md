# MHRS Project Plan

## Project

Multispectral Heart Rate Sensor (MHRS)

## Goal

Design and evaluate a low-cost wearable PPG sensing platform for investigating methods of improving heart-rate measurement reliability during movement.

## Current Research Areas

- Multi-sensor optical sensing
- Multispectral PPG sensing
- Optical and ambient-light shielding
- Motion-aware sensing
- Contact and pressure-aware sensing
- Adaptive sensor selection

Detailed experimental designs, hardware configurations, results, and other project documentation will be maintained in this directory.
